# ZenClip Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

ZenClip does not sell personal information, show advertising, or use analytics or cross-app tracking. Most clipboard history, notes, settings, voice memos, and imported files remain on your Mac unless you choose an optional sync or online feature.

## Information on your Mac

Clipboard items, notes, AI conversations, imported content, cached artwork and lyrics, and voice memos are stored on your Mac. Provider credentials are stored in the macOS Keychain. You can delete these items in ZenClip. macOS may retain application data after removal; you can remove remaining data from your user Library.

## Sync and online features

If enabled, iCloud sync stores supported data in your iCloud account. Notion sync sends the clips and files you select to your connected Notion workspace. AI Chat sends your messages, conversation history, attachments, and, if enabled, relevant clipboard context to the provider or server you select. OpenAI dictation sends the recording to OpenAI for transcription. ZenClip asks for permission before these AI transfers in its App Store build. You can decline a transfer or disconnect the provider at any time. The direct build may use additional features and services you choose to enable.

To provide requested content, ZenClip may send Apple Music searches to Apple, track details to LRCLIB or lyrics.ovh, city and calculation details to AlAdhan, and Quran passage or edition identifiers to the chosen Quran service. Weather searches and forecasts use Open-Meteo. These services receive normal network information such as your IP address. Their handling and retention of data are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. ZenClip's developer does not operate an AI relay server or receive a separate copy of these requests.

## Permissions

Camera, microphone, screen recording, Calendar, Reminders, Accessibility, Apple Music and Automation permissions support the features you choose to use. You can change permissions in macOS System Settings. Accessibility supports paste automation and menu-bar placement.

## Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you delete it or remove the app's data. Delete clips, chats, recordings and imported content in ZenClip, and disconnect providers to remove their saved credentials. For data already sent to a third party, use that provider's retention, deletion, and account controls. Stop or decline use of an online feature to prevent further transfers.

## Contact

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, use the support contact on ZenClip's Mac App Store product page.
